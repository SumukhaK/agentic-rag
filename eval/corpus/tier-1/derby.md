# North London Derby

Arsenal beat Tottenham Hotspur 3-1 at the Emirates Stadium on 15 March 2025. Bukayo Saka scored twice, in the 12th and 54th minutes, and Martin Ødegaard added a third in the 78th minute. Son Heung-min scored Tottenham's only goal in the 63rd minute. The win moved Arsenal to within two points of the top of the table.
